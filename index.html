FAQs About Home Interior Design

An interior designer bring professional space planning, material expertise, and coordination skills to turn your vision into reality. Hiring a designer helps optimize your budget, coordinate contractors, select harmonized colors and materials, and save you from costly trial-and-error mistakes.

Hanswal Interiors is a comprehensive one-stop destination for end-to-end interior design. We offer complete personalization, high-tech modular designs, a flat 10-year warranty, a 45-day move-in guarantee, and a rigorous 146-point quality check process. We manage everything from initial concept and site renovation to delivery and installation, ensuring a smooth, hassle-free experience.

We provide comprehensive solutions covering all aspects of interior transformations, including space planning and 3D modeling, customized modular kitchens and wardrobes, civil works (flooring, tiling, painting), technical installations (plumbing, structural layout, electrical styling), custom false ceilings, custom furniture design, modular partitions, and lighting layouts.

Interior design costs are personalized to each project based on parameters like home configuration (e.g., 2BHK vs 3BHK), material specifications, finish choices (laminate, acrylic, glass), and custom furniture preferences. We offer packages that scale from essential modular setups to premium luxury designs. You can receive a detailed budget estimate based on your specific requirements during our free consultation.

For modular interior installations (such as kitchens and wardrobes), we offer a guaranteed 45-day move-in timeline. Full-home turnkey design projects are typically delivered and installed within 90 days. The timeline is highly dependent on design finalization, site readiness, prompt design feedback, and standard procurement of custom finishes.

Current home design trends focus on wellness, functionality, and organic elements. Popular styles include Japandi (blending Zen minimalism with Scandi warmth), curve detailing (arched structures, curved islands), multi-purpose modular furniture (flexible dividers, built-in study pods), and biophilic design with natural textures, solid woods, and warm earth tones.
